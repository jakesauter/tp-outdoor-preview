How we make it

  1. Saw + laser: one kit per module
  2. Base welded on a fixture so every module bolts to the next
  3. Gate frame, hinge ears and latch
  4. Powder coat, springs, drop-tested 20 times

Why it's built this way

  • Bolt-together modules: 1 gate or 40, no welding at the track
  • One release rod: every gate drops at the same instant
  • Heavy 11 ga tube and 1/4" plate: built for years of race days
  • Spring-driven drop: fast and the same every time
  • Anchors to a concrete pad or stakes into dirt
